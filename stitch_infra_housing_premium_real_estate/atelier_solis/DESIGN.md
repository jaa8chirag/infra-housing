## Brand & Style
The design system articulates the uncompromising precision and luminous clarity of top-tier architectural masterworks. Built for ultra-high-net-worth buyers, architectural connoisseurs, and international property developers, the experience must project serene confidence, structural discipline, and radiant warmth. 

The aesthetic is Modern Architectural Minimalism infused with Technical Precision:
- **Atmosphere**: Bathed in daylight, pristine galleries, honed travertine, cast limestone, and brushed champagne metallic finishes.
- **Visual Discipline**: Strict structural alignments, razor-sharp geometric balance, generous breathing intervals, and meticulous blueprint-inspired annotation systems.
- **Emotional Resonance**: Absolute authority, quiet affluence, crystalline clarity, and permanent architectural value.

## Colors
The palette captures sunlight interacting with raw and refined architectural elements:
- **Crisp Canvas (`#FCFCFA` to `#F7F6F2`)**: Pristine architectural white base with honed French limestone undertones, avoiding cold digital blues in the background.
- **Charcoal Slate (`#1C2024`, Primary)**: Deep basalt and structural steel tone providing razor-sharp legibility and commanding authority for headers and primary framing.
- **Champagne Gold Foil (`#C5A880`, Secondary)**: Matte metallic champagne gold utilized for luxury provenance, insignia, active elevation states, and curated exclusivity markers.
- **Blueprint Cyan (`#007A87`, Tertiary / Telemetry)**: Precise architectural draft cyan for technical annotations, dimensions, floor plan coordinates, square-meter telemetry, and precision micro-indicators.
- **Mortar Slate / Divider Tone (`#E2DFD7`)**: Fine hairlines representing expansion joints and blueprint datum lines.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm relies on three distinct voices:
- **Headlines (Outfit)**: Pure geometric proportions reminiscent of modern monumental inscriptions. Set at light weights (`300`) with tight tracking to evoke bespoke gallery signage.
- **Editorial & Narrative (Manrope)**: Balanced humanist-grotesque engineered for long-form spatial essays, property dossiers, and specification digests.
- **Drafting & Telemetry (Space Grotesk)**: Technical mono-width character set in uppercase with tracked spacing for lot coordinates, scale ratios, elevation heights, and pricing metrics.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a strict 12-column architectural module anchored on a 4px/8px baseline grid. 

- **Desktop (1440px+)**: 12 columns with 32px gutters and generous 64px side borders. Full-bleed photographic panoramic hero panels interlock with off-center technical data columns (e.g., 8-column architectural visualization alongside a 4-column property specification sidebar).
- **Tablet (768px - 1439px)**: 8 columns with 24px gutters and 32px margins. Modules fold into vertical pairings maintaining structural alignment lines.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 16px gutters and 20px edge margins. Floorplans and gallery carousels preserve full-bleed visual impact while metric cards stack neatly.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through planar stratification and daylight cast shadows rather than synthetic heavy drop shadows.

- **Primary Canvas**: Level 0 represents pristine cast plaster (`#FCFCFA`).
- **Limestone Plinths**: Level 1 surfaces (cards, side panels) rest on honed limestone tinted fills (`#F7F6F2`) with hairline architectural boundaries (`1px solid #E2DFD7`).
- **Solar Ambient Shadows**: Elevated overlays (modals, spatial inspection flyouts) utilize soft, high-diffusion daylight shadows: `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(28, 32, 36, 0.06), 0px 4px 16px -4px rgba(28, 32, 36, 0.03)`.
- **Frosted Translucency**: Sticky navigation consoles and inspection bars use `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over `rgba(252, 252, 250, 0.82)` with a bottom baseline border in `#E2DFD7`.

## Shapes
In tribute to international modernist architecture, structural purity requires razor-sharp 90-degree corners (`roundedness: 0`). 

No rounded corners exist across interactive buttons, cards, images, or interactive input fields. Precision is reinforced through pristine right angles, clean geometric miters, and hairline borders, evoking high-precision architectural scale models and blueprint schematics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Acquisition / Inquire)**: Monolithic `#1C2024` charcoal fill, sharp corners, white text (`Manrope`, 13px, uppercase, tracking `0.1em`). Hover initiates a subtle champagne gold boundary reveal: `border: 1px solid #C5A880`.
- **Secondary (Technical Dossier)**: Transparent fill, `1px solid #1C2024`, crisp hover inversion to full `#1C2024` with inverted white text.
- **Tertiary (Telemetry & Interactive Model)**: Clean ghost button with `#007A87` blueprint cyan text, hairline cyan underline on hover, preceded by technical coordinate glyphs (`+`, `[ ]`, `//`).

### Chips & Telemetry Tags
- Rigid rectangular tags with a limestone background (`#F1EFEB`), border `1px solid #E2DFD7`, and text set in `Space Grotesk` uppercase (e.g., `PENTHOUSE 04`, `840 M²`, `SUN EXPOSURE: SW`). Active or highlighted filters toggle to thin champagne borders (`#C5A880`) with champagne-tinted text.

### Form Inputs
- Minimalist architectural baseline field: pristine white or limestone background, bottom border only (`1px solid #1C2024`), or subtle full perimeter hairline (`#E2DFD7`). On focus, the border shifts to `#007A87` with zero offset glow. Floating labels use `Space Grotesk` in muted charcoal (`#62676D`).

### Architectural Cards
- Frameless or fine-line encased (`1px solid #E2DFD7`). Imagery utilizes strict ratios (16:9, 4:3, or 1:1) with a muted daylight grade. Property titles (`Outfit` light) sit above a split technical spec row featuring blueprint-cyan telemetry metrics (living area, terrace exposure, completion quarter). Hover creates a subtle 2px top gold foil accent indicator without shifting layout geometry.

### Floorplan & Telemetry Viewers
- Floating technical HUD elements with translucent backdrop blur, fine crosshair grid markers (`+`), scale rulers, and interactive compass rose dials rendered in Blueprint Cyan (`#007A87`) and Champagne Gold (`#C5A880`).
